# Community Code of Conduct

Sharebox welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

We expect participants to:

- Discuss technical ideas without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's personal information.
- Help keep discussions relevant and accessible to newcomers.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or exclude participants to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate and applied consistently.

To report a concern, contact an OSS Malaysia organization maintainer through the contact channels listed on the [organization profile](https://github.com/ossmalaysia). Do not post sensitive personal information in public reports. Maintainers should handle reports confidentially as far as practical and avoid retaliation against anyone reporting in good faith.
